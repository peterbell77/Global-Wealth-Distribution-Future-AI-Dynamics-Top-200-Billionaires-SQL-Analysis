# Key Insights Summary: Billionaire Wealth Investigation

## Executive Summary
Across the **Top 200 Richest People in the World** dataset, total combined wealth reaches **$6,368.50 billion ($6.37 trillion)**, with an average net worth of **$31.84 billion** and an average age of **69.38 years**. Wealth is heavily concentrated in the **United States** (by both count and aggregate fortune) and anchored in **Technology**, **Finance & Investments**, and **Fashion & Retail**.
